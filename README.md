# MK Designs Studio

Build a premium, modern, highly visual website for an interior design and architecture firm named “MK Designs”.

The website should feel like a high-end interior design studio — elegant, sophisticated, minimal, luxurious, and image-focused. Avoid making it look like a generic corporate website or template.

BRAND

Firm Name: MK Designs
Industry: Interior Design & Architecture
Brand Positioning: Premium, contemporary, personalized interior spaces.

Create a sophisticated visual identity for MK Designs using:

Warm off-white / ivory backgrounds

Charcoal / deep black typography

Muted earthy tones

Subtle gold/brass accents

Large architectural and interior photography

Plenty of whitespace

Elegant serif headings combined with a modern sans-serif body font

The overall design should communicate luxury, craftsmanship, creativity, and attention to detail.

6. DESIGN PROCESS PAGE

Create a timeline-style process.

Understanding the client's lifestyle, requirements, budget and expectations.

02 — Site & Space Analysis

03 — Concept Development

Moodboards, design direction, color palette and material exploration.

04 — Design Development

Layouts, furniture planning, lighting, materials and detailed drawings.

05 — 3D Visualization

Photorealistic renders to visualize the final space.

06 — Execution

Coordination with contractors, craftsmen and vendors.

07 — Final Styling

Furniture, décor, accessories, artwork and finishing touches.

08 — Handover

Final inspection and delivery of the completed space.

Represent this process with beautiful animations and a horizontal/vertical timeline.

FINAL DESIGN DIRECTION

The finished website should feel like the online presence of a premium interior architecture studio, not a normal business website.

Think:

Luxury architecture magazine + contemporary interior studio + high-end portfolio.

Prioritize visual storytelling, photography, typography, whitespace and project presentation.

The most important goal is to make visitors immediately understand:

MK Designs creates beautiful, personalized and professionally executed interior spaces.

Build the website with clean, scalable, maintainable components and a well-organized project structure. Use realistic placeholder content and imagery where actual MK Designs content is not yet available, while making everything easy to replace later.
